## What doc-kit is

**doc-kit** turns a web application into **one self-contained HTML file** of documentation. The file opens offline in
any browser and holds everything: annotated screenshots with numbered markers, guided tours, full-text search
([[key Ctrl+K]]), a glossary with tooltips, SVG diagrams, light and dark themes, and printing of the whole site.

You write the pages in Markdown, you describe the screenshots in small capture plans, and the kit does the rest: it
signs in with your session, captures the screens **read-only**, measures the zones, builds the site and checks it.

> [!TIP] This site is an example
> This documentation is itself a doc-kit project (`docs/en` in the kit's repository). Its screenshots come from the
> kit's fictional demo application, **Acme Orders**, and from this very site.
